# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Pinned by the client brief: React + TypeScript + Vite, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui structure (`@/` alias, `src/components/ui`), Framer Motion, Lucide React, Spline runtime (`@splinetool/react-spline`) and WebGL where needed.

## Users

All four groups are confirmed customers:

- Talleres y mecánicos: buy by part name or reference, often daily, need fast answers.
- Transportadores / camiones: fleets and heavy vehicles (air brakes, suspension, bolts).
- Industria: hydraulics, hoses, industrial fasteners.
- Conductores particulares: parts for their own vehicle, often unsure of the exact reference.

## Product Purpose

The official website of TORNIRACORES 2, a physical store that sells fasteners (tornillería), automotive spare parts, mechanical components, tools and related solutions. The site must help a visitor find the part they need and request a quote, and connect the digital experience with the real store.

Success = quote requests sent through WhatsApp with enough data (product/reference, vehicle, quantity) for the store to answer.

## Positioning

A real, recognisable corner store (black and industrial-yellow façade, product families painted on its pillars: herramientas, retenedores, suspensión, kit de clutch, pernos, tornillería, mofles) brought online. Its claim is "Calidad y Servicio".

## Operating Context

Quotes are handled by the store over WhatsApp. There is no backend, cart, or online payment.

## Product Principles

1. Finding the part and asking for a quote beats spectacle; every effect must serve that.
2. Nothing fake: no invented data, no buttons that pretend to work, no simulated sends.
3. The real store is the proof — show it.
4. Every placeholder is labelled and replaceable from one data file.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mixed audience on phones in workshops and on the road: mobile-first, large touch targets, keyboard support, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
